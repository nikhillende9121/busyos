IMPS ONE
Inventory, Purchasing & Point-of-Sale Platform for Multi-Store Retail Businesses


THE PROBLEM
============================================================

Most growing retail and distribution businesses run into the same wall:

- Stock counts on the shelf don't match stock counts on paper. Nobody
  can say, with confidence, exactly how many units of a product exist
  right now across all stores and warehouses.

- Purchasing, billing, and stock tracking live in separate tools (or
  separate spreadsheets), so every purchase or sale has to be entered
  twice, and the two records drift apart over time.

- Every store or branch prices and discounts differently, with no
  central control and no record of what was actually charged once a
  promotion changes.

- Staff have access to more than their job requires, simply because
  giving everyone the same login was easier than setting up proper
  access levels.

- Owners and finance teams can't see a live, trustworthy view of
  stock, sales, and purchases across all their locations at once -
  only after someone compiles it, days later.

- Adding a new store or a new staff member means new manual setup,
  new spreadsheets, and new room for mistakes.

None of these are staffing problems. They are the predictable result
of running a multi-location business on tools that were only ever
built for a single till, a single shop, a single ledger.


WHAT IMPS ONE PROVIDES
============================================================

IMPS One is a single platform that runs purchasing, inventory, and
sales together, across as many stores and warehouses as the business
operates - so stock, pricing, and money always tell the same story.

- ONE STOCK COUNT, EVERYWHERE
  Every purchase, sale, transfer, and adjustment updates the same
  live stock ledger. What the system shows on screen is what is
  actually on the shelf, in every warehouse, all the time.

- PURCHASING TO SELLING, IN ONE FLOW
  Products, purchase orders, supplier bills, and point-of-sale are
  one connected system, not three tools stitched together by hand.
  A purchase received updates stock the moment it's confirmed; a
  sale reduces it the moment it's rung up.

- CENTRAL CONTROL OVER PRICING AND PROMOTIONS
  Prices can be set per store, per customer tier, or per individual
  customer from one place, and every discount or coupon that was
  ever applied to a sale is kept exactly as it was charged - even
  after the promotion itself is later changed.

- ACCESS THAT MATCHES THE JOB
  Every staff member gets a role - Cashier, Store Manager, Purchase
  Officer, Admin, or a custom role built for the business - and can
  only do what that role permits. A cashier cannot edit prices; a
  purchase officer cannot issue refunds. This is enforced by the
  system, not by trust.

- A REAL-TIME VIEW FOR OWNERS AND FINANCE
  Stock levels, sales, purchases, and GST-ready reports are visible
  live, across every store, without waiting for a month-end
  reconciliation.

- BUILT-IN POINT OF SALE
  A counter-ready POS with cash-drawer session tracking (opening
  float, sales through the shift, closing count) so every shift
  reconciles itself against what the till actually took in.

- NEW STORES, MINUTES NOT WEEKS
  Onboarding a new store or branch is a guided setup, not a rebuild -
  new locations are live on the same system, with the same reporting,
  the same day.

- YOUR DATA, YOUR EYES ONLY
  Every business on the platform is fully walled off from every
  other. Nothing about one company's stock, prices, sales, or
  customers is ever visible to another.


WHO THIS IS FOR
============================================================

- Retail chains and franchises running more than one store
- Distributors and wholesalers managing stock across warehouses
- Businesses currently juggling separate billing, inventory, and
  spreadsheet systems that no longer agree with each other
- Any growing business that has outgrown a single-till, single-shop
  way of working


WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
============================================================

Most inventory tools are built for one shop and stretched to cover
more. IMPS One is built the other way around: multi-store and
multi-warehouse from day one, so a business scales from one location
to fifty without switching systems, re-training staff, or losing
history along the way.

Every action - a purchase, a sale, a stock adjustment, a price
change - is recorded permanently. Nothing is silently overwritten.
That record is what makes the live stock count trustworthy, the
audit trail complete, and the reporting something the business can
actually stand behind.


IN SHORT
============================================================

IMPS One replaces disconnected billing, spreadsheets, and guesswork
with one system where purchasing, stock, and sales always agree -
across every store the business runs, on day one and at scale.
